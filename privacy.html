WEBSITE INFORMATION

PRIVACY POLICY.

Effective: October 19, 2026 · Pre-publication draft — business review recommended

This policy explains how MAB Auto Styling handles information you provide when you request a service quote from our website. We operate in Dallas, Texas and surrounding areas.

Information we collect

Our quote form requests your full name, email address, vehicle year/make/model, selected services, and optional phone number and message. If you choose the optional SMS and calls checkbox, we also store your selection and the exact consent statement. We record the date/time of your request. Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical information to protect the form against abuse.

How we use your information

We use submitted details to review requested services, prepare quotations, arrange appointments, contact you about your request, prevent spam, and maintain records related to providing our services. We do not require consent to text messages or calls in order to submit the form.

SMS and calls

The consent checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. If you opt in, you authorize service-related SMS or calls to the number you provide. Message frequency varies and standard rates may apply. Reply STOP to a business text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also contact us using the email address below. No automated marketing texts are sent by the website itself.

Storage and service providers

When the secure quote system is configured, requests are stored using Cloudflare D1, and Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify form submissions. If enabled, an email service provider sends a copy of each request to our business inbox. We share data with service providers only as needed to process your request and operate the website. No credit-card or payment data is collected by the form.

Retention and choices

We retain quote information only as long as reasonably needed for customer service, operational records, and applicable legal requirements. You can ask about the information associated with your request or request deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements, by emailing us.

Contact

For privacy questions email mabautodetail@gmail.com or call (469) 389-0809. Business: MAB Auto Styling, Dallas, Texas and surrounding areas.

We may update this policy to reflect operational changes. The effective date above will change if we update it.
